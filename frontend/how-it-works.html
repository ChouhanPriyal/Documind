How DocuMind Works

DocuMind transforms PDFs and image documents into structured intelligence. Upload regulations, contracts, or scanned documents, and run OCR, RAG Q&A, AI Summaries, Keyword Extraction, and Document Comparison.

📤

1. Upload Document

Upload any PDF document, PNG, JPG, or JPEG file. DocuMind supports files up to 20MB.

🔍

2. Hybrid OCR & Text Extraction

Native PyMuPDF text extraction combined with Tesseract & Gemini Vision OCR reads digital and scanned pages accurately.

💬

3. Multi-Page RAG Q&A

Ask specific questions (e.g. "What is the minimum attendance requirement?") and get grounded answers citing exact page numbers.

⚡

4. AI Summarization

Generate executive summaries, key takeaways, critical numbers & requirements, and action item checklists with one click.

🏷️

5. Keyword & Entity Extractor

Automatically extract named entities, regulations, numerical rules, topics, and frequency terms.

⚖️

6. Document Compare & Reports

Compare two documents side-by-side to highlight differences, and download Markdown, HTML, or TXT reports.

💡 Pro Tips for Best Results

  • Clear Scans: Upload high-resolution images or standard PDF files for peak OCR accuracy.
  • Specific Questions: Ask precise questions like "What is the minimum attendance requirement?" or "What is the late submission fee?" for targeted page citations.
  • Interactive Tags: Click any extracted keyword chip to instantly question the document about that topic.
  • Download Reports: Use the Export & Reports tab to download full Markdown or HTML intelligence reports for offline review.
